This is a concrete reason to use event-body evidence and never infer a date from a URL slug. No 2026 occurrence was verified. Keep the tradition as a research lead; do not create a 2026 event from it.

The library scrapers do not discover these neighborhood posts. The scheduled core-weekend script imports already-entered leads; it does not conduct web/social research. This establishes a coverage gap, but not the precise historical failure stage for this event: no run log was available proving whether it was never found, filtered, or rejected.

The user-supplied Thursday extras file explicitly identifies itself as a mockup not wired to cron. Its September dates and default looked marks are not current source-check evidence. The operating search instructions still used six weeks for one-offs, unlike the 90-day library horizon. - [Niles Canyon Train of Lights](https://www.ncry.org/ride/train-of-lights/): selected dates Nov 20–Dec 30, but organizer says all 2026 tickets sold out, updated Oct 3. Watch releases; not a book-now pick.
- [Los Altos Festival of Lights Parade](https://losaltosparade.org/): strong fit, but direct organizer source could not be read. Participant listing says Nov 29, 2026 while referring to a 2025 parade. Verify official current date before publishing.
- Sunnyvale downtown tree lighting: previous 2025 city listing is not confirmation for 2026. Current-year source needed.
- Sunnyvale Breakfast with Santa and Noon Year’s Eve: earlier discovery reports identify candidates, but current details were not independently reverified in this pass. Keep on follow-up, do not reuse old report as proof.
- [Cal Academy ’Tis the Season](https://calacademy.org/tis-the-season-for-science): reviewed page says the event is closed and includes old animal dates. Watch for a new 2026 announcement.
- Greenmeadow Holiday Open House: membership restriction and no confirmed 2026 date. Not a general-public recommendation.
- Independent Hanukkah, Las Posadas, Kwanzaa and Thanksgiving programs: continue research with OFJCC, libraries and cultural organizers. Fairy Winterland advertises multiple winter traditions, but that does not establish independent events or dates for each holiday.
- Skip the local Holiday Bazaar unless a meaningful 0–5 activity is verified; shopping alone is insufficient. Gym holiday survival nights are drop-off, not caregiver-child holiday outings.
